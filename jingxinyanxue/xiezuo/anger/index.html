静心研学 - 写作 - 续写情绪之愤怒


1. anger相关

01. 他的话里充满了愤怒。
There was much anger in his words.
02. 她试图掩饰自己的愤怒。
She tried to hide her anger.

2. rage相关

03. 他的暴怒让周围的人害怕。
His rage frightened the people around him.
04. 她在暴怒中摔碎了花瓶。
She smashed the vase in a rage.

3. madness相关

05. 战争的残酷会带来疯狂。
The cruelty of war can bring madness.
06. 他在疯狂中做出了错误决定。
He made a wrong decision in madness.

4. fury相关

07. 他的眼睛里闪烁着狂怒。
There was fury in his eyes.
08. 敌人的攻击激起了我们的狂怒。
The enemy's attack aroused our fury.

5. angry相关

09. 他因为迟到而生气。
He is angry because he is late.
10. 妈妈生气地看着调皮的孩子。
Mother looked at the naughty child angrily.

6. annoyed相关

11. 他对持续的噪音感到气恼。
He is annoyed by the continuous noise.
12. 她因被误解而气恼不已。
She is extremely annoyed by being misunderstood.

7. enraged相关

13. 不公平的对待让他暴怒。
He was enraged by the unfair treatment.
14. 看到破坏的场景他暴怒起来。
He became enraged when seeing the scene of destruction.

8. irritated相关

15. 频繁的打扰让他恼怒。
He is irritated by the frequent interruptions.
16. 她因计划被打乱而恼怒。
She is irritated because the plan is disrupted.

9. mad相关

17. 他在堵车时抓狂得大喊。
He shouted madly during the traffic jam.
18. 考试失败让他抓狂。
He went mad because of failing the exam.

10. furious相关

19. 他对这个结果狂怒不已。
He is furious about the result.
20. 她因被欺骗而狂怒。
She is furious because she is deceived.
