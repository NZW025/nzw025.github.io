宁归处

静心研学 - 写作 - 续写情绪之焦虑

一. 担忧 (concern)
01. 我们对环境的担忧与日俱增。
Our concern about the environment is growing.
02. 她的担忧在眼神中清晰可见。
Her concern is clearly visible in her eyes.
二. 焦虑 (anxiety)
03. 他的焦虑源于工作压力。
His anxiety comes from work pressure.
04. 考试前的焦虑让他难以入眠。
Anxiety before the exam makes it hard for him to sleep.
三. 忧虑 (agitation)
05. 她脸上的忧虑无法消散。
The agitation on her face won't disappear.
06. 他带着忧虑处理棘手问题。
He deals with the tough problem with agitation.
四. 担忧的 (concerned)
07. 那位担忧的父亲四处寻找孩子。
The concerned father is looking for his child everywhere.
08. 担忧的医生密切关注病人。
The concerned doctor pays close attention to the patient.
五. 焦虑的 (anxious)
09. 焦虑的投资者等待着股市消息。
Anxious investors are waiting for stock market news.
10. 焦虑的心情让他坐立不安。
The anxious mood makes him restless.
六. 焦虑不安的 (agitated)
11. 焦虑不安的人群呼喊着口号。
The agitated crowd shouts slogans.
12. 他焦虑不安的状态很明显。
His agitated state is very obvious.
七. 坐立不安的 (restless)
13. 坐立不安的他在房间里走来走去。
Restless, he walks around in the room.
14. 坐立不安的思绪困扰着他。
Restless thoughts trouble him.
八. 紧张的 (nervous)
15. 紧张的运动员做着深呼吸。
The nervous athlete takes deep breaths.
16. 她紧张的样子让人有些心疼。
Her nervous look makes people feel a bit sorry.
九. 颤抖 (shake)
17. 他冷得直颤抖，赶紧穿上外套。
He shakes with cold and quickly puts on a coat.
18. 地震时大地颤抖，房屋晃动。
The earth shakes during the earthquake and houses sway.
十. 惶恐 (panic)
19. 突发的警报让人群陷入惶恐。
The sudden alarm threw the crowd into panic.
20. 她强压着内心的惶恐面对危机。
She suppressed her inner panic to face the crisis.
＜ 续写情绪之愤怒 ＞ 情绪之恐惧
