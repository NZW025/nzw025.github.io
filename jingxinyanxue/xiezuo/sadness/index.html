沈罗记

静心研学 - 写作 - 情绪之悲伤

一. sadness相关
01. 他的眼神中充满了悲伤。
There is full of sadness in his eyes.
02. 失去亲人让她陷入深深的悲伤。
Losing her family member threw her into deep sorrow.
二. misery相关
03. 贫困的生活给他带来了痛苦。
The poor life brought him misery.
04. 他对自己的错误充满了悔恨。
He is full of regret for his mistake.
三. depressed相关
05. 长期的失业让他感到沮丧。
The long - term unemployment made him depressed.
06. 比赛的失败令他失望。
The failure of the game brought him disappointment.
四. desperation相关
07. 被困在沙漠中使他陷入了绝望。
Being trapped in the desert, he was in desperation.
08. 多次尝试无果让他遭遇挫折。
Many fruitless attempts brought him frustration.
五. upset相关
09. 听到这个消息他心烦意乱。
He was upset when hearing the news.
10. 她对结果感到失望。
She was disappointed with the result.
六. heartbroken相关
11. 恋人的离去让她心碎。
She was heartbroken because of her lover's leaving.
12. 计划的受阻让他受挫。
He was frustrated by the block of the plan.
七. low-spirited相关
13. 糟糕的天气让他情绪低落。
The bad weather made him low - spirited.
14. 陷入绝境的他感到绝望。
Being in a desperate situation, he felt desperate.
八. regretful相关
15. 他对过去的行为感到悔恨。
He was regretful for his past behavior.
16. 小女孩因找不到妈妈哭了起来。
The little girl cried because she couldn't find her mother.
九. weep相关
17. 她为逝去的青春而哭泣。
She wept for the lost youth.
18. 他因伤心事而抽泣不止。
He sobbed because of the sad thing.
十. choke相关
19. 她激动得哽咽说不出话。
She choked with excitement and couldn't speak.
20. 他因压力大而经常叹气。
He often sighed because of great pressure.
十一. frown相关
21. 他因忧愁而皱眉。
He frowned because of worry.
＜ 情绪之惊讶 ＞ 情绪之喜悦
