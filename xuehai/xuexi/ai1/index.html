➂ Rebuild teachers

Learn to make requests to AI and give it scripts. (Keling AI, iFlytek Zhizuo, Tencent Zhiying, Jimeng, Youyan). Return to the essence of subject teaching. Taking beauty retouching as an example, AI has replaced PS.

Note the difference between digital literacy and intelligent literacy. The concept of digital literacy: the ability to effectively and responsibly find, understand, evaluate, create and communicate information in a digital environment, transforming from a tool to a partner.

Its framework dimensions: digital awareness, digital technical knowledge and skills, digital application, digital social responsibility, and digital professional development.

Intelligent literacy, however, has brought educational technology into an era of equality, enabling innovation in this era. The connotative characteristics and framework evolution of intelligent literacy: its connotative characteristics are intelligent collaboration ability, ethical navigation ability, critical evaluation ability and innovative design ability (see picture below).



The competence framework includes people-oriented thinking, ethical foundation and application, pedagogy, and promotion of professional development.

The AI era has promoted the development from digital literacy to intelligent literacy. In terms of educational technology in teaching, it has transformed from instrumental rationality to value rationality. Teachers' roles have changed from knowledge transmitters to learning designers, technology users to intelligent collaborators, and ethical guides to innovation catalysts. The transformation from digital literacy to intelligent literacy has realized the change from human-machine systems to dual-teacher teaching.

What is AI dual-teacher? It is to construct a cognitive subject system of HT (Human Teacher) plus AI-T (Artificial Intelligence Teacher), forming a teaching community through brain-computer collaboration, knowledge complementarity and ability superposition.

How to do well in AI dual-teacher? Make good division of labor and form a four-dimensional collaborative mechanism: cognitive dimension, role dimension, decision-making dimension and space-time dimension (see picture).



2025 is the first year of smart education. Artificial intelligence will endow teachers with new roles and missions, liberate teachers from simple labor, enable them to have more time for creative teaching activities, and focus on core educational tasks such as temperament cultivation and value shaping.
